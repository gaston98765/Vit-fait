🤝 Vit’fait: Connecting Students with Opportunities
Bridging the gap between student talent and local needs. Vit’fait is a platform designed to solve two problems at once: helping university students monetize their free time around their class schedules and providing local clients with a trusted pool of help for small tasks.

✨ Key Features

🎓 For Students: Monetize Your Free Time * Find Flexible Work: Browse, filter, and view local tasks that fit your specific schedule.

Apply & Track: Easily apply for tasks and view your complete task history in one place.

Dual Roles: Students can also act as clients by posting their own tasks when they need help.

💼 For Clients: Find Reliable Help

Post in Minutes: Create and post new tasks with clear descriptions for students to see immediately.

Manage Applicants: Review student profiles and assign the task to the best candidate.

Track Progress: Monitor the status of your assigned tasks from start to finish.

🛡️ Secure & Professional Infrastructure

Role-Based Authentication: Secure login system with separate permissions for students and clients.

Dedicated Dashboards: Custom, user-friendly interfaces tailored to each user type.

Full Task Management: Transparent lifecycle management ensures all applications and tasks are handled clearly.
